Zero Trust Security Architecture

Trust & Privacy Shield

Your census data is protected by client-side WebAssembly C++ cryptography. Sensitive identity markers (Aadhaar, DOB) are salted and hashed locally on your device before transmission.

Never Asks for Bank OTPs or PINs

Census 2027 will NEVER request financial credentials, banking passwords, or OTP verification.

Local WASM C++ Edge Encryption

All PII data is SHA-256 encrypted directly inside your browser sandbox using C++ Wasm modules.
